## Integrated conclusion

The source specialist and runtime/cost specialist outputs are compatible when findings are scoped correctly: the source auth contract can be implemented by the narrow local Worker seam and a cost model can evaluate the Paid hypothesis, but neither establishes full API parity, actual provider CPU behavior, D1 operational query counts, R2 lifecycle parity, or a payable monthly ceiling. Research preserves the timing, malformed URI, full validator, CSRF/rate-limit, data/concurrency and billing uncertainties. A2-F1/F2 now have explicit nonzero route/data/CPU drivers in the A3 candidate and mutation checks guard against their omission. An independent cost-method critic recalculated all nine scenario/sensitivity points and recorded a bounded PASS; the result and its constraints are in `.orchestration/evidence/critics/i01-cost-method-critic.md`. No specialist approved its own work.

### A3-F1 QA mutation rework (Artifact A4 candidate)

The A3 external critic found that the old delete-prefetch mutant zeroed all R2 Class B operations and did not test the actual missing-prefetch defect. The A4 candidate now enforces `imageDeleteObjectReads === routeDrivers.imageDelete × r2ClassBGetObjectBeforeFreeDeletePerOperation`, pins the per-operation factor to 1, and asserts the pinned source map retains `GetObject before free DeleteObject`. Its coherent negative mutant removes only delete-prefetch reads, preserves public and ADMIN GET counts, and recomputes the aggregate plus billing rounding. The validator rejects it specifically at the source-bound route/factor invariant; the correct scenario data and cost calculator are unchanged. Focused independent QA review evidence is included with the A4 candidate.

No API/source product code, schema migration, runtime deployment, remote Cloudflare resource, spending/billing setting, production URL, or real data is part of the integrated candidate. Cost sensitivity assumes all logins reach bcrypt when deriving CPU scenarios; unknown-account logins skip bcrypt and burst concurrency is outside the monthly-volume model.
